Tests

Timed practice tests that end with an estimated level. Not official ratings.

Listening and reading run entirely in your browser. Speaking is graded by Claude using your own API key, which never leaves your browser except to Anthropic. See a graded example first: sample result (illustrative).

Built inside my private study app from August 2026; published here as a standalone copy on 29 September 2026.

You hear each Portuguese recording once, then answer three questions from memory and your notes.

10 or 15 recordings · about 45 minutes

10 or 15 passages · 5 minutes each

Speaking test

14 prompts · about 25 minutes
