How it works

A plain-English summary. The longer write-up is METHOD.md in the repository.

Listening and reading

Each test is a walk through blocks of five passages at one level, with three multiple-choice questions per passage. The walk starts at Advanced Low. A strong first block sends you up a level, a weak one sends you down. After two blocks, or three when you hit the top or bottom of the range, two neighbouring blocks (30 questions) are added up and looked up in a score table to give a level from Novice Low to Superior. Listening plays each recording once, with no replay, and gives you a preview of the questions before and a short review after. Reading gives a fixed five minutes per passage and does not let you go back.

How the passages are pitched to levels

Every passage was written to target one level, then rated blind by several AI raters who saw only the text or transcript, never the intended level. Passages were revised until the raters agreed with the intended level about 80% of the time and were never more than one step away. Questions were then checked with a second blind test: an AI answering with the questions and choices but without the passage. The first draft banks scored 91% that way, far too high, so questions were rewritten until the banks fell inside the 50 to 70% target where they could.

Speaking and writing

You answer spoken or typed prompts. Claude rates the whole sample the way a human rater would: it looks for the level you can sustain across the routine questions (the floor), and for where you start to break down on the harder questions (the ceiling). Rehearsed answers are discounted, and the test form you chose in the self-assessment caps the highest level that can be printed. Practice sittings also give a level for each answer and a list of grammar and word-choice mistakes.

What this is not

These are practice tests. No human raters have validated them, the passages are AI-written and the audio is synthetic, and the levels are estimates. Inspired by the ACTFL Proficiency Guidelines. Not affiliated with or endorsed by ACTFL; results are not official ratings.
